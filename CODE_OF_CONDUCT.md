# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The short version is below; the linked text is the one that applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Disagree about ideas, with evidence. This project asks for numbers; bring
  them, and be ready to be shown different ones.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them.
- Remember that other people's setups, languages and constraints differ from
  yours.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
  This includes transcripts and prompts of sessions that are not yours.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@eduardopin](https://github.com/eduardopin), through the contact options on
that GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently.
